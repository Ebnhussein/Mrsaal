The real challenge

Why we built Mrsaal

Applying for work takes time. We built Mrsaal to simplify repetitive communication tasks while keeping your own voice and judgement at the centre.

01. Friction

Less time lost to repetition

Copying text, changing company names and editing each recipient by hand is tiring and makes avoidable mistakes more likely.

With Mrsaal: Import your contact list and organise the details before preparing messages.

02. Chaos

Keep your follow-ups organised

Scattered records make it hard to remember when you sent a message, whether a reply arrived and what to do next.

With Mrsaal: A central activity log keeps each contact and send result in one place.

Messages that sound like you

Our design principles

The foundations behind Mrsaal

A practical assistant, with clear boundaries and realistic expectations.

front_hand Principle 01

You make the decisions

Choose your companies, upload your CV and review the words and recipients before sending. Scheduled messages are sent only at the time and through the channel you choose.

check_circle Send only after your approval
smart_toy Principle 02

AI supports your writing

AI helps turn your CV and instructions into a useful draft. It can make mistakes, so every suggestion stays available for you to review and edit.

check_circle Keep your own voice
verified_user Principle 03

Respect and privacy first

Use appropriate contacts and avoid unwanted messages. Gmail connects through Google OAuth. Review the privacy policy to understand stored data and what your chosen AI provider receives.

Clear expectations

What Mrsaal does

Understand the purpose and limits of the tool before you start.

check

Mrsaal helps you

  • done_all Organise and follow up: Manage the companies and organisations you choose to contact.
  • done_all Draft with AI: Prepare a message based on your experience and the company details you provide.
  • done_all Contact directly: Send through your connected Gmail account or WhatsApp. Delivery and spam filtering depend on the receiving service.
  • done_all Track your activity: Review send dates and results to plan your next steps.
Make more time for interview preparation and developing your skills.
close

What to expect

  • block Not a job board: Mrsaal does not list vacancies or promise a job offer.
  • block Bring your own company list: We do not supply scraped or leaked email databases.
  • block No guaranteed outcomes: Replies and hiring decisions are up to the companies you contact.
  • block Responsible sending: Use relevant contacts and respect the policies of your connected services.
