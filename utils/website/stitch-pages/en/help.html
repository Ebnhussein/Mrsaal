Mrsaal uses Google OAuth 2.0 to connect Gmail without asking for your Google password. Review Google's consent screen for the requested permissions, including sending messages and reading email to detect replies. You can revoke access from your Google account settings. Connecting does not guarantee inbox placement.
Check the account status in Mrsaal and the linked devices in WhatsApp. If the session has been logged out, pair it again using the new QR code. Delivery can depend on WhatsApp and the receiving device.
Provider keys are stored encrypted on the server when the required encryption configuration is enabled. The project uses AES-256 for encryption. You can remove or replace a key from settings. Never include a key in a support message.
Connected services set their own limits. Use relevant contacts, review the messages and follow the provider's policies. A delay does not guarantee delivery or prevent spam classification. Stop and investigate if the provider returns quota or account errors.
